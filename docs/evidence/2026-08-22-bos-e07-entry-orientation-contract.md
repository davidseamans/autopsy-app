# BOS-E07 — Entry Paths, Orientation and Maturity Contract

Date: 22 August 2026  
Status: Draft contract evidence; no public release or Tenant activation

## Implemented contract

- The public conversation identifies Apprentice Business Owner or Established Business without silently starting an assessment, account or Tenant.
- Discover return access requires verified identity through the existing identity boundary.
- Apprentice progression to Control requires a Pass, First 5 Jobs completion, evidence and voluntary acceptance.
- Established Business direct entry bypasses Apprentice but not qualification.
- Direct-entry qualification requires an authorised representative, operating business, valid versioned jurisdiction, customer-approved reconciled QBO company, customer-managed conversion, standard connection boundary, supported operations and evidence.
- Tenant creation is permitted only after the relevant entry decision is complete.
- Established businesses receive a standard 90-day Orientation, with an earlier review permitted when evidence justifies it and no special pricing treatment.
- Control 1–2 and Manage 1–3 recommendations remain provisional, evidence-based and explicit about benefits and costs.

## Reused boundary

The implementation does not create a second candidate identity or password system. Existing passwordless candidate claim/resume and business identity controls remain authoritative.
