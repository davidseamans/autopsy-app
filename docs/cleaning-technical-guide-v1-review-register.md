## Production controls

- Every ordinary pathway identifies the surface, location and products already used before giving a process.
- Unknown products, mixed products, uncertain surfaces and visible damage route to a plain-language stop.
- No procedure recommends improvised chemical mixing.
- Labels and safety data sheets remain authoritative for product use, personal protection, dilution, ventilation and first aid.
- Images are reference examples, not a diagnosis.
- The interface uses short, mobile-first choices and keeps internal rubric or governance language away from the apprentice user.

## Independent review required before certification

For each pathway, an appropriately competent reviewer must record:

- supported surface and soil combinations;
- prohibited products, mixtures and conditions;
- required personal protection, ventilation and dwell-time controls;
- whether a small hidden-area test is required;
- stop and escalation wording;
- source edition or publication date;
- approval date, reviewer scope and next review date.

Until that record exists, the interface must retain the `Source-backed draft` status and its review note.
