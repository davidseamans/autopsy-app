# Cleaning Technical Guide — Shower Review Batch 01

Status: Expert-review checkpoint. Not production-approved treatment guidance.

This batch converts the approved shower diagnostic prototype into five governed outcomes:

1. ordinary organic and soap film;
2. white mineral-like marks;
3. limited mould-like surface spotting;
4. shower seals, tracks and corners; and
5. damage, uncertainty or unknown previous chemicals — stop and escalate.

## Field expert review

For each of the first four procedures, confirm or correct:

- what an experienced cleaner calls the condition;
- what a novice commonly mistakes it for;
- which surfaces the procedure is suitable for;
- the tools normally used;
- the order of work;
- what must never be used or done;
- what satisfactory completion looks and feels like;
- what indicates permanent staining or damage;
- when the cleaner must stop; and
- the plain-language explanation given to the customer.

Record alternatives and contradictions. Do not force agreement where methods legitimately differ.

## Novice test

Give the mobile guide to a person who has not seen it before and ask them to resolve:

- greasy film on known glass with nothing previously applied;
- white marks on an uncertain surface;
- dark spotting with widespread moisture damage;
- debris in an intact track; and
- a surface with an unknown chemical already present.

Pass criteria:

- the ordinary routes reach the intended draft procedure;
- every uncertain or unsafe route stops;
- the tester can state the expected outcome and stop conditions;
- no tester believes the guide authorised product mixing; and
- the process can be completed comfortably on a phone.
